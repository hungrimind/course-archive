# The Power Grid

Electricity is generated at **power plants**, then immediately stepped *up* to hundreds of thousands of volts. Why? Higher voltage means less energy lost as heat during long-distance travel through **transmission lines**.

Before it reaches your home, **transformers** at substations step the voltage back *down* in stages: from hundreds of thousands of volts, to thousands, to the 120V or 230V at your wall outlet.

## Fill in the Blanks

Fill in the blanks:

```
Electricity is generated at power plants, then stepped up to __(1)__ for long-distance travel. This reduces energy lost as __(2)__ during transmission. Before reaching your home, __(3)__ step the voltage back down in stages to the 120V or 230V at your wall outlet.
```

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. `high voltage`
2. `heat`
3. `transformers`

</details>

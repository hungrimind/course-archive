# Breakers and Grounding

**Circuit breakers** monitor current flow. If too much current rushes through a circuit (say from a short circuit or overloaded outlet), the breaker *trips* and cuts power instantly. This protects your wiring from overheating and starting fires.

Pair that with **grounding**, which routes stray current safely into the earth, and you have your home's two-layer safety net. Breakers stop overcurrent; grounding stops *you* from becoming the path.

## Quiz

**1. What does a circuit breaker do when too much current flows through a circuit?**

- A) It increases resistance to slow the current down
- B) It trips and cuts power instantly
- C) It redirects excess current into the ground wire
- D) It sends an alert to the power company

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) It trips and cuts power instantly

When a circuit breaker detects excessive current from a short circuit or overloaded outlet, it trips and cuts power instantly to prevent wiring from overheating and starting fires.

</details>

**2. What is the primary purpose of grounding in your home's electrical system?**

- A) To make circuits run more efficiently
- B) To reduce your electricity bill
- C) To route stray current safely into the earth instead of through you
- D) To increase the voltage available at outlets

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- C) To route stray current safely into the earth instead of through you

Grounding provides a safe path for stray current to flow into the earth, preventing you from becoming the path for fault current.

</details>

**3. What are the two layers of your home's electrical safety net?**

- A) Fuses and surge protectors
- B) Circuit breakers and grounding
- C) Transformers and insulators
- D) Hot wires and neutral wires

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) Circuit breakers and grounding

Circuit breakers stop overcurrent (too much current flowing) and grounding stops you from becoming the path for stray current. Together they form a two-layer safety net.

</details>

**4. What danger does a circuit breaker primarily protect against?**

- A) Lightning strikes
- B) Voltage spikes from the power grid
- C) Wiring overheating and starting fires
- D) Power outages

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- C) Wiring overheating and starting fires

When too much current flows through a circuit, the wiring can overheat and start a fire. Circuit breakers detect this overcurrent and cut power before that can happen.

</details>

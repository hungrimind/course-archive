# Inside Wall Outlets

A standard outlet has three wires. The **hot wire** carries AC voltage into your device. The **neutral wire** completes the circuit back to the grid. And the **ground wire** is pure safety: it does nothing during normal operation.

If something goes wrong and the hot wire touches a metal case, the ground wire gives current a safe shortcut to earth *instead of through you*.

# Mains Voltage Kills

Your wall outlets deliver **120V** (North America) or **230V** (most of the world) of **AC power**. Either is *far* more than enough to push lethal current through your body.

> [!danger]
> Never open electrical panels, modify wall outlets, or work on mains-powered circuits as a beginner. This is not hobby territory; it requires trained electricians.

## Quiz

**1. What type of power do standard wall outlets deliver?**

- A) DC power at low voltage
- B) AC power at 120V or 230V
- C) AC power at 12V
- D) DC power at 230V

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) AC power at 120V or 230V

Wall outlets deliver AC (alternating current) power at either 120V (North America) or 230V (most of the world), both of which are far above safe levels.

</details>

**2. Why should beginners never work on mains-powered circuits?**

- A) The components are too expensive to replace
- B) Mains voltage is more than enough to push lethal current through your body
- C) Mains circuits are too complicated to understand
- D) It voids your home insurance automatically

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) Mains voltage is more than enough to push lethal current through your body

Mains voltage (120V or 230V) can easily push enough current through a human body to kill. This work requires trained electricians, not beginners.

</details>

**3. Which of the following activities should you NEVER do as a beginner?**

- A) Build a circuit on a breadboard with a 5V USB supply
- B) Connect an LED to a battery with a resistor
- C) Open electrical panels or modify wall outlets
- D) Program an Arduino board

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- C) Open electrical panels or modify wall outlets

Opening electrical panels and modifying wall outlets involves mains voltage, which is extremely dangerous. These tasks require trained electricians.

</details>

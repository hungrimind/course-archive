# Basic Safety Rules

Always use **low-voltage power supplies** (USB or batteries). Disconnect power before changing any wiring. Keep drinks away from your workspace. And the big one: *never* connect a hobby circuit to mains power.

> [!tip]
> Respect electricity, but don't fear it. At 3.3V or 5V, you're in a safe playground, so experiment freely.

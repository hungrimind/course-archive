# Calculating Power

The power formula is **P = V x I** which means, voltage times current gives you watts. Just like Ohm's Law, one simple multiplication.

A 5V USB port delivering 2A provides 5 x 2 = **10W**. This tells you how much energy your circuit is consuming and whether your power supply can keep up.

## Ohm's Law Practice

**1.** Given voltage = 5, current = 2, solve for **power** (W).
Hint: P = V × I

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

10 W

5V × 2A = 10W

</details>

**2.** Given voltage = 12, current = 0.5, solve for **power** (W).
Hint: P = V × I

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

6 W

12V × 0.5A = 6W

</details>

**3.** Given voltage = 3.3, current = 0.02, solve for **power** (W).
Hint: P = V × I

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

0.066 W

3.3V × 0.02A = 0.066W (66mW)

</details>

**4.** Given voltage = 230, current = 10, solve for **power** (W).
Hint: P = V × I

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

2300 W

230V × 10A = 2300W

</details>

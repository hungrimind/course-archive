# The One Formula

**Ohm's Law** states that voltage equals current times resistance: **V = I x R**. That's it -- one formula connects all three quantities you learned about.

Know any two and you can find the third. *Think of it like a triangle: cover the value you want, and the other two show you the math.* This single relationship is the foundation of nearly every circuit calculation you'll ever do.

## Ohm's Law Practice

**1.** Given voltage = 6, resistance = 300, solve for **current** (A).
Hint: I = V ÷ R

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

0.02 A

6V ÷ 300Ω = 0.02A (20mA)

</details>

**2.** Given current = 0.05, resistance = 100, solve for **voltage** (V).
Hint: V = I × R

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

5 V

0.05A × 100Ω = 5V

</details>

**3.** Given voltage = 12, current = 0.003, solve for **resistance** (Ω).
Hint: R = V ÷ I

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

4000 Ω

12V ÷ 0.003A = 4000Ω (4kΩ)

</details>

# Solving for Resistance

Need to know what resistor to use? Rearrange Ohm's Law one more time: **R = V / I**. Divide the voltage you need to drop by the current you want, and you get the resistance.

This is the version you'll reach for most often in practice. *You usually know how much voltage you have and how much current something needs -- the resistor value is the missing piece.*

## Ohm's Law Practice

**1.** Given voltage = 5, current = 0.02, solve for **resistance** (Ω).
Hint: R = V ÷ I

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

250 Ω

5V ÷ 0.02A = 250Ω

</details>

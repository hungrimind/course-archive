# Atoms All Around

Everything you can touch, breathe, or stand on is made of **atoms**: tiny building blocks far too small to see, even with most microscopes.

Each atom has a dense center called the **nucleus**, packed with protons and neutrons. Orbiting that nucleus are even tinier particles called **electrons**. *Think of it like a solar system in miniature*, a heavy core with lightweight particles zipping around it.

## True or False

**1. True or false:** Electrons are found inside the nucleus of an atom.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**False**: Electrons orbit outside the nucleus. It is protons and neutrons that are packed inside the nucleus.

</details>

**2. True or false:** Atoms are the building blocks that make up all physical matter you can touch or stand on.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**True**: Everything around us (solids, liquids, and gases) is composed of atoms, even though they are far too small to see with the naked eye.

</details>

**3. True or false:** The nucleus of an atom contains protons and neutrons.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**True**: The nucleus is the dense center of an atom and is packed with two types of particles: protons (positively charged) and neutrons (no charge).

</details>

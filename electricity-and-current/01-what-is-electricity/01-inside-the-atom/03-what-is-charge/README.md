# What Is Charge?

**Charge** is an invisible force built into matter. Protons carry a **positive** charge, electrons carry a **negative** charge, and neutrons have none.

The one rule: *opposites attract, likes repel.* A positive proton pulls a negative electron toward it, while two electrons shove each other away. This push-and-pull between charges is the force that makes electrons move, and moving electrons are what we call electricity.

## True or False

**1. True or false:** Two electrons placed near each other will attract each other.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**False**: Like charges repel. Since both electrons carry a negative charge, they push each other away. Only opposite charges (positive and negative) attract.

</details>

**2. True or false:** Neutrons carry no electrical charge.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**True**: Of the three main subatomic particles, only protons (positive) and electrons (negative) carry charge. Neutrons are electrically neutral, which is where their name comes from.

</details>

**3. True or false:** The push and pull between positive and negative charges is what makes electrons move.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**True**: The attractive force between opposite charges and the repulsive force between like charges are what drive electron movement, which is the foundation of electricity.

</details>

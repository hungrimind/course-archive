# Conductors Let It Flow

A **conductor** is any material whose outer electrons move freely between atoms. Give those electrons a push and they pass easily from one atom to the next, like a crowd doing the wave.

Metals, especially **copper** and **aluminum**, are excellent conductors. That's why electrical wires are made of metal: the electrons inside them flow with very little resistance.

## Fill in the Blanks

Fill in the blanks:

```
A plumber choosing pipe material is like an electrician choosing wire material. The plumber picks smooth, open pipes so water flows easily. Similarly, an electrician picks __(1)__ wire because it is an excellent __(2)__, meaning its outer electrons move freely with very little __(3)__.
```

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. `copper`
2. `conductor`
3. `resistance`

</details>

# Insulators Block the Flow

An **insulator** is a material that grips its electrons tightly, making it very hard for current to pass through. Where conductors let electrons hop freely, insulators keep them locked in place.

**Rubber**, **plastic**, and **glass** are common insulators. That plastic coating on every wire? It's an insulator keeping the electricity on the metal path inside and away from your fingers.

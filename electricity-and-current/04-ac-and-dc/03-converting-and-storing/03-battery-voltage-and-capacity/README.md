# Battery Voltage and Capacity

**Voltage** tells you how hard a battery pushes electrons. A standard AA gives **1.5V**, a lithium cell gives **3.7V**, and a 9V battery gives -- you guessed it -- **9V**. Different devices need different push.

**Capacity**, measured in **milliamp-hours (mAh)**, tells you how *long* that push lasts. A 2,000 mAh battery can deliver 2,000 mA for one hour, or 200 mA for ten hours. Higher mAh means more runtime before the chemical reaction runs out.

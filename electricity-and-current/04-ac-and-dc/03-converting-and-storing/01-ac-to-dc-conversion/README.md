# AC to DC Conversion

Your wall outlet delivers AC, but your phone needs DC. That chunky block on your charger cable is solving this exact problem.

Inside it, a **rectifier** flips the negative half of the AC wave to positive, so the current only flows one way. Then a **smoothing circuit** irons out the bumps into a clean, steady DC output. Every laptop adapter, phone charger, and USB power brick does this same conversion.

## Fill in the Blanks

Fill in the blanks:

```
When you plug in a gaming console, the power brick first uses a __(1)__ to flip the negative half of the AC wave so current flows only one way. Then a __(2)__ removes the remaining bumps to produce clean, steady __(3)__ output.
```

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. `rectifier`
2. `smoothing circuit`
3. `DC`

</details>

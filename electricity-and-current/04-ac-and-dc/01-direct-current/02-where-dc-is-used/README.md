# Where DC Is Used

Almost *everything* with a chip runs on **DC**. Your phone, laptop, LED lights, USB gadgets, game controllers -- all of them need that steady one-direction flow to work.

That is because transistors and microchips are designed for constant, predictable voltage. DC is the language that circuits speak, which is why batteries and USB ports deliver it directly.

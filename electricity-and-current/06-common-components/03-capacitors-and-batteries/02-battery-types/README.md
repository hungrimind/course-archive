# Battery Types

**Alkaline** batteries (AA, AAA, 9V) are cheap and available everywhere, but once they're dead, you toss them. **Lithium-ion** batteries are rechargeable and pack a lot of energy into a small space -- that's what powers your phone and laptop.

**Coin cells** are the tiny flat ones inside watches, remotes, and key fobs. Each battery type trades off voltage, capacity, size, and rechargeability, so the right choice depends on what your circuit actually needs.

# What Capacitors Do

A **capacitor** stores a small amount of electrical energy and releases it quickly -- like a tiny rechargeable bucket. It charges up when voltage is applied and discharges the moment that voltage drops.

This makes capacitors great at smoothing out voltage ripples, filtering electrical noise, and providing quick bursts of power. The flash in a camera, for example, uses a capacitor to dump stored energy into the bulb all at once.

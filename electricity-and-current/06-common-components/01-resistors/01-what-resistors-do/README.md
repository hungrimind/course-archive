# What Resistors Do

A **resistor** limits how much current flows through a part of your circuit. Think of it like a narrow section in a water pipe -- it doesn't stop the flow, it just *restricts* it.

Without resistors, sensitive components like LEDs would receive too much current and burn out instantly. By placing a resistor in front of them, you control the flow to a safe level.

Resistors are the single most common component in electronics. Nearly every circuit you'll ever see has at least one.

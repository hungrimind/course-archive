# What Are Potentiometers?

A **potentiometer** is a resistor you can adjust. Turn a knob or slide a lever, and the resistance changes smoothly from zero up to a maximum value.

The volume knob on a stereo is a classic example -- turning it changes the resistance in the audio circuit, which controls how much current reaches the speaker. More current means louder sound, less current means quieter. It's just a variable resistor with a friendly interface.

# How Motors Spin

An electric **motor** converts electrical energy into spinning motion. Inside, a coil of wire sits between permanent magnets. When current flows through the coil, it creates its own magnetic field that pushes against the permanent magnets, forcing the shaft to rotate.

Reverse the current and the motor spins the other direction. This basic principle drives everything from tiny fans in your laptop to the wheels of an electric car.

## Quiz

**1. What does an electric motor convert?**

- A) Light energy into sound energy
- B) Electrical energy into spinning motion
- C) Heat energy into electrical energy
- D) Chemical energy into light

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) Electrical energy into spinning motion

An electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical spinning motion, which can then be used to drive fans, wheels, and other mechanisms.

</details>

**2. What creates the magnetic field that makes a motor's shaft rotate?**

- A) A battery strapped to the shaft
- B) Friction between moving parts
- C) Current flowing through a coil of wire inside the motor
- D) The permanent magnets spinning on their own

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- C) Current flowing through a coil of wire inside the motor

When current flows through the coil of wire inside the motor, it creates its own magnetic field. This field pushes against the permanent magnets, forcing the shaft to rotate.

</details>

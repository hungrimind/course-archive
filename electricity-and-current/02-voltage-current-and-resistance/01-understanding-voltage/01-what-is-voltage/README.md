# What Is Voltage?

**Voltage** is electrical pressure: the force that pushes electrons through a wire. *Imagine a water hose: voltage is the water pressure behind it.* Higher voltage means a stronger push.

Without voltage, electrons just sit still. They need that pressure difference to start moving, the same way water won't flow through a hose unless something is pushing it.

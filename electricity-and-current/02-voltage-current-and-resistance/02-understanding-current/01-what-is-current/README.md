# What Is Current?

**Current** is the flow of electrons through a conductor, specifically how many pass a given point each second. *If voltage is the water pressure, current is how much water is actually flowing.*

More electrons moving means more current. A thin trickle of electrons is a small current; a flood of them is a large one.

# Measuring Resistance

Resistance is measured in **ohms**, represented by the Greek letter omega (**\u03A9**). A short thick copper wire might have less than 1\u03A9, while a resistor component could have 1,000\u03A9 or more.

> [!tip] Large values get shorthand: 1,000\u03A9 = 1 **kilohm (k\u03A9)** and 1,000,000\u03A9 = 1 **megohm (M\u03A9)**.

## Quiz

**1. What unit is used to measure resistance?**

- A) Volts (V)
- B) Amperes (A)
- C) Ohms (Ω)
- D) Watts (W)

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- C) Ohms (Ω)

Resistance is measured in ohms, represented by the Greek letter omega (Ω).

</details>

**2. How many ohms are in 1 kilohm (kΩ)?**

- A) 100Ω
- B) 1,000Ω
- C) 10,000Ω
- D) 1,000,000Ω

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) 1,000Ω

The prefix 'kilo' means one thousand, so 1 kΩ equals 1,000Ω.

</details>

**3. A resistor is labeled 2.2 MΩ. How many ohms is that?**

- A) 2,200Ω
- B) 22,000Ω
- C) 220,000Ω
- D) 2,200,000Ω

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- D) 2,200,000Ω

The prefix 'mega' means one million, so 2.2 MΩ equals 2,200,000Ω.

</details>

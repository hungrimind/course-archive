# What Is Resistance?

**Resistance** is anything that opposes the flow of electrons. *Think of it as a narrow section in a pipe: the water still flows, but the squeeze slows it down.*

Every material has some resistance. Copper has very little, which is why it's used for wiring. Rubber has a lot, which is why it's used for insulation. Nothing conducts perfectly.

# Analog Around Us

Temperature, sound, and light intensity are naturally analog: they change smoothly, never snapping between fixed steps. The real world *is* analog.

Sensors that measure these things produce analog voltage signals that mirror those smooth changes. A thermometer chip outputs a gently rising voltage as the room warms. A microphone's voltage traces the shape of the sound wave hitting it. The signal is a tiny electrical copy of the real thing.

# Sampling Analog to Digital

To turn an analog signal into a digital one, a circuit **samples** the voltage: it measures it many times per second and records each measurement as a number.

The more samples you take per second, the more accurately the digital version mirrors the original smooth signal. This is exactly how a microphone records sound digitally: the smooth sound wave is measured thousands of times per second, and each snapshot becomes a number a computer can store.

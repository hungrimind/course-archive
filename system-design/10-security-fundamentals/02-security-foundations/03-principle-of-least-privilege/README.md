# Principle of Least Privilege

The **Principle of Least Privilege** ensures that users and systems have only the access they need to perform their tasks, and nothing more. This minimizes the risk of accidental or malicious misuse of system resources.

```plaintext
[Admin Access] -> [Read-Only Access] -> [No Access]
```

Limit permissions to the minimum necessary, avoiding broad access that could lead to security vulnerabilities.

> [!warning]
> Overlooking this principle can lead to unauthorized access and potential data breaches.

## Quiz

**1. What is the main goal of the Principle of Least Privilege?**

- A) To ensure users have maximum access to resources
- B) To minimize accidental or malicious misuse of resources
- C) To provide equal access to all users
- D) To simplify user management by providing generic access

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) To minimize accidental or malicious misuse of resources

The Principle of Least Privilege aims to minimize the risk of misuse by ensuring users and systems have only the access necessary for their tasks.

</details>

**2. Which of the following best describes how permissions should be assigned according to the Principle of Least Privilege?**

- A) Broad permissions to ensure task completion
- B) Permissions based on group rather than individual roles
- C) Minimal necessary permissions to perform tasks
- D) Permissions should be the same for all users

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- C) Minimal necessary permissions to perform tasks

Permissions should be limited to what is necessary for the task to reduce security vulnerabilities, in line with the Principle of Least Privilege.

</details>

**3. Which of the access levels best represents the Principle of Least Privilege?**

- A) Admin Access
- B) Read-Only Access
- C) No Access
- D) All Access

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) Read-Only Access

Read-Only Access represents a practical example of granting just enough access to complete a task, aligning with the Principle of Least Privilege.

</details>

**4. What is a potential consequence of overlooking the Principle of Least Privilege?**

- A) Increased system responsiveness
- B) Unauthorized access and potential data breaches
- C) Improved user satisfaction
- D) Simplified permissions management

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) Unauthorized access and potential data breaches

Failure to adhere to the Principle of Least Privilege can lead to unauthorized access and data breaches due to unnecessarily broad access.

</details>

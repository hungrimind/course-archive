# Design Tradeoffs

A **design tradeoff** is a choice between competing options, each with its own benefits and drawbacks, in system design. Balancing these tradeoffs is essential to meet specific goals like performance, scalability, or cost.

> [!tip]
> Consider both short-term needs and long-term growth when evaluating tradeoffs.

## Quiz

**1. What is a design tradeoff?**

- A) A compromise between options with benefits and drawbacks.
- B) A decision that only focuses on cost reduction.
- C) A choice that always prioritizes short-term benefits.
- D) An option without any disadvantages in system design.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- A) A compromise between options with benefits and drawbacks.

A design tradeoff involves choosing between competing options, each with its own pros and cons, to achieve specific goals.

</details>

**2. Why should both short-term needs and long-term growth be considered in design tradeoffs?**

- A) To ensure quick scalability without additional costs.
- B) To balance immediate performance needs with future expansion potential.
- C) To eliminate all future design challenges.
- D) To avoid making any compromises.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- B) To balance immediate performance needs with future expansion potential.

Considering both short-term needs and long-term growth ensures that a system can meet current demands while being prepared for future requirements.

</details>

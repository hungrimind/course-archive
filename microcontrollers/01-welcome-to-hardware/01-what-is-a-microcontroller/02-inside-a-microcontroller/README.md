# Inside a Microcontroller

A microcontroller packs three things onto one chip: a **processor**, **memory**, and **I/O pins**.

The processor runs your code. The memory stores your program and its variables -- but we're talking kilobytes, not gigabytes. The I/O pins are the real star: they're the physical connection points where your chip talks to the outside world.

*Think of it like a brain (processor), a notepad (memory), and hands (I/O pins) -- all shrunk onto a chip smaller than your thumbnail.*

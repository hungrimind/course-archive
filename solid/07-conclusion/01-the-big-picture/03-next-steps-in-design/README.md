# Next Steps in Design

After mastering the SOLID principles, the journey doesn't stop. **Design patterns** are the next logical step. These are tried-and-true solutions to common design problems, like templates that solve specific issues.

> [!tip]
> Start with patterns like Singleton, Factory, and Observer, since these are foundational and widely used.

**Continuous learning** is key. Follow blogs, join forums, and read books on software design to stay updated. The field evolves, and so should your skills.

**Apply SOLID principles** in real-world projects. Look for opportunities to refactor codebases you work on. This reinforces learning and shows tangible benefits.

> [!note]
> Practice makes perfect. Regularly review and reflect on your design choices to deepen your understanding.

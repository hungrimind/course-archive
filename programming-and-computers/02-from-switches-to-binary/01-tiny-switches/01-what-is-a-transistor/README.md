# What is a Transistor?

A **transistor** is a tiny electronic switch inside your computer. It can be either ON or OFF, nothing in between.

Think of it like a light switch. You flip it up (ON) or down (OFF). There's no halfway. A transistor does the same thing, except it switches using electrical signals instead of your finger. This simple ON/OFF behavior is the foundation that everything else in computing is built on.

## True or False

**1. True or false:** A transistor can be halfway between ON and OFF, like a dimmer switch.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**False**: A transistor acts as a strict ON/OFF switch with no in-between state. It is either fully ON or fully OFF.

</details>

**2. True or false:** A transistor is controlled by electrical signals rather than by a person physically flipping it.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**True**: Unlike a wall light switch that you flip by hand, a transistor is switched ON or OFF by electrical signals, which is what allows computers to operate so fast.

</details>

**3. True or false:** A transistor is a tiny mechanical switch with moving parts inside the computer.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**False**: A transistor is an electronic switch, not a mechanical one. It has no moving parts, it uses electrical signals to switch between ON and OFF.

</details>

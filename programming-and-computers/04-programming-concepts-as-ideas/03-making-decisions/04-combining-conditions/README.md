# Combining Conditions

You can glue conditions together with **AND** and **OR**.

**AND** means both must be true: *if it's Saturday AND sunny, go to the park.* One rainy Saturday and the whole thing is false. **OR** means at least one must be true: *if you have a ticket OR you're on the guest list, come in.* Either one is enough.

This is how programs express the same kind of compound logic you use in everyday decisions.

## Matching

Sort each item into the right category: **AND (both must be true)**, **OR (only one needed)**

- Be 18+ AND pass the test to drive
- Have a ticket AND a passport to fly
- Have a ticket OR be on the guest list
- It's Saturday AND sunny for a picnic
- Pay with cash OR card
- Speak English OR Spanish to apply

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**AND (both must be true)**
- It's Saturday AND sunny for a picnic
- Have a ticket AND a passport to fly
- Be 18+ AND pass the test to drive

**OR (only one needed)**
- Pay with cash OR card
- Have a ticket OR be on the guest list
- Speak English OR Spanish to apply

# Next Steps

In the next lesson, you will be able to get your certificate of completion for this course. But before we go, we want to guide you to the next steps you can take to continue learning and building.

## Build

A course is the initial part of your journey; the next part is creating production-ready software. From here on out, the most important thing you can do is start building apps.

As a member you have access to the [Flutter Kit](/kit/flutter) which is built upon the knowledge you gained in this course. You can use it and our other products to build good apps.

### Some App Ideas

The best idea is the one you are passionate about. So, if you have something in mind, **go for it**.

If you are unsure what to build, you can keep iterating on the apps we built in this course.

Here is the code and some ideas.

[Todo App](https://github.com/hungrimind/flutter_todo/tree/main/steps/final)

- Add a due date to the todo item
- Add a category to the todo item
- Add a note to the todo item
- Add a priority to the todo item

[Workout App](https://github.com/hungrimind/workout/tree/main/final)

- Customize the workout with your exercises
- Add a chart to show your progress visually
- Support weights, reps, and duration

The beautiful thing about coding is you can build anything you want.
